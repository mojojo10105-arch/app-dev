# My Favorite Series: Game of Thrones

![Game of Thrones Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/d/d8/Game_of_Thrones_title_card.jpg)

## Overview
**Game of Thrones** is an epic fantasy series based on George R. R. Martin's *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels.  
It tells the story of noble families fighting for control of the **Iron Throne** and the **Seven Kingdoms of Westeros**, while ancient enemies return from beyond the Wall.

## Why I Love It
- Deep and complex characters  
- Unexpected plot twists  
- Stunning visuals and epic battles  
- Powerful quotes and emotional moments  

> “When you play the game of thrones, you win or you die.” — Cersei Lannister

## Main Characters
1. Jon Snow  
2. Daenerys Targaryen  
3. Tyrion Lannister  
4. Arya Stark  
5. Robb Stark

## My Favorite Moment
The **Battle of the Bastards** — one of the most intense and cinematic battle scenes ever shown on TV.
